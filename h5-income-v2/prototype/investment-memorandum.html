01 / GEX USDT INCOME

Product overview

A fixed-term USDT income product within the GEXFI account experience.

GEX USDT Income is designed for eligible customers who purchase USDT through GEXFI and wish to allocate a portion of their balance for a defined period. The product has three terms: 90 days, 180 days and 365 days. Subscriptions, income illustrations and redemption amounts are denominated in USDT.

The product connects income holdings with GEXFI Wallet and, separately, eligible card funding services. An Income holding is not available wallet cash and cannot be spent directly using a card. At maturity, the redemption process returns the payable amount to the customer’s GEXFI Wallet before any further transfer or card funding transaction.

Target returns are objectives, not a guarantee. The amount ultimately payable may be lower than an illustration, and capital may be lost. This is not a bank deposit or a Tether-issued income product.

Who should consider the product?

Customers should be able to leave the subscription amount invested for the full term and accept investment and digital-asset risks. The product is not designed for emergency funds or balances needed for near-term spending. Eligibility remains subject to account approval, product availability and applicable service restrictions.

04 / GEX USDT INCOME

Income calculation & fees

The displayed annual targets already reflect the 0.50% p.a. product management fee.

Illustrative income = principal × net annual target × term days ÷ 365

For 1,000 USDT over 180 days, the illustration is 1,000 × 4.50% × 180 ÷ 365 = 22.1917808 USDT, displayed as 22.19 USDT. A daily target-equivalent calculation is an accrual illustration, not a promise of a daily cash payment.

Income records and settlement

Holdings may display target accruals and subsequently confirmed income. The interface and statements must distinguish estimated amounts, confirmed amounts and amounts paid. Seeing a number on a daily chart does not mean that the same amount can be withdrawn that day. The settlement statement distinguishes the target illustration from the realized result, identifies any shortfall and shows the actual amount payable. Income is settled with the holding at maturity under the subscription terms; a daily record is not a separate daily distribution.

“Net target” means after the stated product management fee. It does not mean after all purchase, exchange, transfer, card or personal tax costs. No claim is made that this product is fee-free.

A separate service charge requires its own disclosed quote; it is not silently deducted as another management fee. 06 / GEX USDT INCOME

Maturity & redemption

Redemption follows maturity and settlement; it is not an on-demand withdrawal.

The 90-day, 180-day and 365-day products are intended to be held for their selected calendar-day term. There is no flexible product in this range. The accepted subscription confirmation records the start and maturity dates and the applicable redemption instruction.

01

Maturity reached

The holding enters its maturity settlement process. An elapsed term does not, on its own, create available wallet funds.

02

Income and payable amount confirmed

The settlement statement records principal, actual income, applicable disclosed deductions and the net redemption amount.

03

Wallet credited

The payable USDT amount is credited to GEXFI Wallet once settlement and required checks are complete. A processing record is not a completed credit.

04

Funds used or reinvested

After credit, the customer can select available wallet, transfer or card services. A new subscription requires its own accepted instruction.

Settlement timing

The timing and business-day convention applicable to an order are supplied before confirmation and retained with that order. The account displays the expected settlement date and progress. Maturity does not imply T+0 payment, and an internal wallet credit does not mean that a bank or card has also been credited.

Early exit and cancellation

An instruction can only be cancelled before acceptance where the account permits it. Accepted fixed-term holdings are not available for ordinary early withdrawal. Any exceptional exit permitted by law or accepted under the agreement is assessed separately; the quoted income adjustment and disclosed costs require the customer’s acceptance. No secondary-market buyer or platform repurchase is promised.

Delays, failed settlement and renewal

A required verification, account restriction, disputed instruction or execution failure may interrupt settlement. The customer record preserves the amount, reason and status until resolution; a failed transaction is not labelled completed. Customer communications follow the applicable agreement. No automatic renewal is assumed: reinvestment requires a new instruction or an express mandate, with the new term and rate disclosed in advance.

07 / GEX USDT INCOME

Wallet, card & digital records

Connected services, with separate balances and a clear transaction record.

Wallet first, card second

At the end of the redemption process, funds return to GEXFI Wallet. Card funding is optional and separate. A customer is not required to load a card in order to receive a redemption to the wallet. Card availability, limits and acceptance depend on the relevant card programme.

Before a card funding transaction, the customer should be shown the funding asset, quoted rate, applicable charges, amount to be credited, quote validity and expected processing terms. USDT-denominated holdings are not automatically USD-denominated card money, and no 1:1 conversion is assumed.

RecordWhat it establishes

On-chain evidence

If a blockchain record is made available, the network, transaction hash, record type and confirmation status should be identified. A receipt does not independently establish ownership of every underlying asset, the solvency of the issuer or a guaranteed investment value.

A digital receipt does not, by itself, create a tradable investment token, free transferability or a secondary market. Any verification link must correspond to an actual record on the stated network. No sample hash is evidence of a live transaction.

Security and corrections

Customers should verify instructions through their account and report discrepancies promptly. Corrections to records should preserve the original transaction trail and explain the change. Public blockchain records must not be treated as a substitute for the investment agreement.
