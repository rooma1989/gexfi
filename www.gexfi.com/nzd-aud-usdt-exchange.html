Exchange questions

Can I exchange NZD or AUD for USDT?

Sign in to check the NZD/USDT or AUD/USDT direction enabled for your account and its final quote. Public examples do not guarantee availability.

Are these rates guaranteed for my order?

No. The board refreshes GEXFI customer rates, while the final quote, fees and validity are shown on your order before confirmation.

When does the order complete?

Order submission, verified receipt, execution and delivery are separate stages. Check the final status in the order record.

Can a business use the same account route?

Business OTC has separate KYB and access approval. See the business page for its process.

How are exchange fees and settlement confirmed?

The order displays the customer quote, currency, fees and validity before confirmation. Funding receipt is verified separately from submitting a receipt; the final status is in the order record.
