VISA CARD FAQ

Details to check before using your card.

For card availability, limits, fees and binding status, refer to the current information in your GEXFI account.

Which payment apps can I add the Visa card to?

Eligible cards may be added to Apple Pay, Google Pay, WeChat Pay and Alipay. Specific card, device, merchant and regional restrictions are determined by each platform and issuer.

Can I use it for both online and in-store purchases?

The card is intended for eligible online and in-store payments. Acceptance depends on the specific card, payment method and merchant.

Can I top up my Visa virtual card with USDT?

Where your account supports it, review the USDT exchange route and then the separate card funding order. On-chain wallet, GEXFI account and card balances do not transfer automatically.

Does USDT in my on-chain wallet automatically load onto my card?

No. The on-chain wallet, GEXFI account balance and Visa card balance are distinct. Follow the available account exchange and funding steps to load a card.

Where can I check the funding amount and fees?

Review the funding order before submission. The order page shows the applicable amount, currency, fees and estimated credited amount for your available route.

Where are my card transactions shown?

Sign in to GEXFI and open the Visa card area to see available balance, funding and card activity records.
