Frequently asked questions

GEXFI product and service FAQs

The account page shows the features and exchange routes available to you.

Can I exchange NZD or AUD for USDT?

Open the exchange screen to see your currently supported currency pairs, quote and order terms before submitting.

Does GEXFI provide receiving accounts?

Available receiving account applications appear in the account area. Eligibility and account details are confirmed during the application.

Can I fund and use a GEXFI Visa card?

Open the card area to check your available Visa card, funding route and fees. Supported cardholders can use eligible mobile wallets, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, WeChat Pay and Alipay, where available.
